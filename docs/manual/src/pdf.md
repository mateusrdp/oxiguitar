# Exporting a PDF

**File → Export PDF…** saves the score as a PDF, next to the song unless you choose another folder.

- It contains the tracks shown on screen, with the notation each one shows.
- It is always laid out in pages (the Page layout), whichever layout the window uses, and printed in black on white even with Dark Page on. Every voice is black: the grey of voices other than the caret's is only on screen.
- **File → Paper Size** chooses A4 or Letter.
- The PDF uses vector graphics and embedded fonts, so it prints sharply at any size.

To print, open the PDF in your PDF viewer and print from there.
