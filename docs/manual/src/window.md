# The window

From top to bottom:

- **Menus**: File, View, Tools, Help.
- **Toolbar**: buttons in groups. File: Open, Save, Export PDF. Edit: Undo, Redo. View: Dark Page. Save, Undo and Redo are greyed out until the editor arrives. **View → Toolbar** hides it. Plugins will add their own groups.
- **Top panel**: the **Player** tab, with play, play selection, pause, stop and rewind, count-in, loop and metronome, and the choice of output (how the score will sound). They stay greyed out until an output plugin is installed ([Settings and plugins](plugins.md)).
- **Left panel, the score, right panel.** The left panel will hold the palette of notation tools and the right panel the properties of what you select, both with the editor; today they hold a note saying so and start folded away. The score sits on a dark background whatever the page colour, with its zoom slider in the corner ([Zoom](zoom.md)).
- **Bottom panel**: the **Tracks** tab ([Tracks and notation](tracks.md)) and the **Mixer** tab, which arrives with the player.
- **Messages**: one line at the very bottom with the latest message: what went wrong opening a file, what a tool did, what is missing. Click it to see every message since OxiGuitar started; the chevron in its corner folds the list back.

## Panels

Each panel has tabs. Click a tab's name to show it; click the name of the tab that is already showing, or the chevron at the end of the tab strip, to fold the panel away to its strip, and again to bring it back. A folded side panel is a thin strip: click it to open it. Drag the edge between a panel and the score to resize it.

**View → Panels** hides or shows each panel altogether (F7 for the left one, F8 for the bottom one). Plugins will be able to add tabs to any panel.
