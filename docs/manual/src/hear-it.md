# Hear it

A few of OxiGuitar's own reference songs, played by OxiGuitar 0.9 exactly as they sound in the window. They were rendered with `oxiguitar-cli play SONG --wav OUT`, with no effects or editing afterwards, and then encoded as MP3. The guitars use FreePats' Clean electric guitar (CC0) and everything else uses FluidR3_GM (MIT), both from [Better sounds](better-sounds.md).

**A band**: three guitars, bass, drums, piano, strings and a voice, each track at its file's volume and pan.

<audio controls preload="none" src="sounds/band.mp3"></audio>

**Bends**: full, half and released bends on a guitar, each moving only its own string.

<audio controls preload="none" src="sounds/bends.mp3"></audio>

**Strums and techniques**: strums up and down, slap, pop and tapping.

<audio controls preload="none" src="sounds/strums.mp3"></audio>

**Drums**: General MIDI's kit.

<audio controls preload="none" src="sounds/drums.mp3"></audio>

These songs are short pieces written to test OxiGuitar, not music. Amp modelling (NAM), which turns a plain guitar into a real amp's sound, comes with OxiGuitar 1.0, and so will examples of it here.
