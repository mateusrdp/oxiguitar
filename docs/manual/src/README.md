# OxiGuitar

OxiGuitar is a free guitar tablature and score viewer for Windows, macOS and Linux. Version 0.1 opens Guitar Pro files, shows them as standard notation and tablature, and exports them to PDF. Editing and playback come in later versions.

![The OxiGuitar window with a score in the Page layout](images/window.png)

The window has menus at the top, the score in the middle, the track list along the bottom and the score zoom at the bottom right. Everything else is in the menus or on a key: see [Keyboard and mouse](keys.md).

**Privacy.** OxiGuitar never connects to the internet on its own and never sends anything anywhere: no telemetry, no accounts, no automatic crash reports.

Found a problem, or something missing from this manual? Open an [issue](https://github.com/mateusrdp/oxiguitar/issues) or ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/mateusrdp/oxiguitar/discussions).
