# Public test fixtures

Small files that are safe to redistribute: made by us for OxiGuitar, or carrying a license that allows it. CI runs on every one of them. Each file is listed here with its origin and license.

Files of unclear ownership never go here; see `corpus/README.md`.

The three test songs hold the same music: a repeat, a marker, a time-signature change, a triplet, a hammer-on, a bend, a tie, a dead note, a rest, a chord and (in GP5) a second voice. The Guitar Pro 6 and 7 songs add a rehearsal letter, a chord diagram, a strum and a tempo change in the middle of a bar. To regenerate the fixtures after changing a writer: `OXIGUITAR_BLESS=1 cargo test -p oxiguitar-formats --test guitar_pro --test gpif`.

## Guitar Pro 7.6's own files

`guitar-pro-7.6/` holds ten reference songs as Guitar Pro 7.6 saved them (their score XML only) and two files Mateus made in Guitar Pro 7.6 for OxiGuitar (a guitar, and pianos with very low and high notes), which show how Guitar Pro writes pitches and instruments without strings. See its README. Made by Mateus for OxiGuitar; GPL-3.0-or-later.

## Files for checking the .gp writer

`guitar-pro-check/` holds what the .gp writer makes of every reference song and of the test song in every format, for anyone to open in Guitar Pro or another program (see its README and "Checking what OxiGuitar writes" in `CONTRIBUTING.md`). Made by us; GPL-3.0-or-later. Regenerate with `scripts/guitar-pro-check.sh`.

## Visual-regression references

`events/` holds the timeline of each reference song and of the test song, as `oxiguitar-cli events` prints it: the player's golden files (`crates/oxiguitar-play/tests/golden.rs`). Made by us; GPL-3.0-or-later. Regenerate after an intended change with `OXIGUITAR_BLESS=1 cargo test -p oxiguitar-play --test golden`, and read the diff.

`reference/` holds the fifty visual-regression references: Guitar Pro 7 files generated by `crates/oxiguitar-testgen/src/references.rs` and the pictures they engrave to. `reference/README.md` lists them. Made by us; GPL-3.0-or-later. Regenerate after an intended change with `OXIGUITAR_BLESS=1 cargo test -p oxiguitar-testgen --test visual` (see `docs/testing.md`).
